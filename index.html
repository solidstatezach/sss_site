OF MANY · CREATIVE LIFE ACTION NETWORK

Beadwork, story, and scholarship in service of community.

Of Many is the work of Rita Esmeralda Naranjo, M.A.—weaving together hand-made beadwork, trauma-informed practice, and decolonizing education for youth, families, and communities.

Each piece, workshop, and gathering is part of a larger effort to honor our ancestors, protect our youth, and reconnect people to land, culture, and each other.

ABOUT

Rita Esmeralda Naranjo, M.A.

I grew up in the foster care system and in community with other Indigenous youth navigating institutions that were never built for our thriving. My work is dedicated to healing those fractures—through storytelling, beadwork, teaching, and community organizing.

I am Muisca/Carib, originally from Tamesis, Antioquia, Colombia. Over the last two decades I have served as a mentor, advocate, trainer, and lecturer, holding space for foster youth, parents, students, and community members to remember their inherent worth and reconnect to culture, land, and story.

Of Many emerged as a creative life action network: a way to weave together art, cultural restoration, trauma-informed practice, and community care.
